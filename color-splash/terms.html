2. Description of Service

The App is a credit-based tool available within the Canva platform that allows users to:

  • Select an image on their Canva design
  • Optionally describe which subject to keep in color
  • Apply a color splash effect that keeps the subject in color while converting the background to grayscale
  • Preview and add the processed image to their design
