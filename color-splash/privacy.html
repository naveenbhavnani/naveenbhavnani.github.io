Privacy Policy

Color Splash

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Overview

Color Splash ("the App") is a Canva app developed by Naveen Bhavnani that transforms images so the main subject stays in color while the background becomes grayscale.

Summary: The image you choose is sent to our server only to apply the effect, and it is not stored. To keep track of credits, the App stores your Canva user ID alongside your credit balance. Payments are handled by Gumroad, and we never see your card details.

Information We Collect

The App collects only what it needs to process images and run its credit system:

  • Your Canva user ID, an anonymous identifier provided by Canva. It is not your name or email address.
  • Your credit balance, and how many credits you have used and purchased.
  • A history of credit changes, such as a credit used for an image, a credit returned after failed processing, or credits added by a purchase.
  • Purchase references: the Gumroad order ID and the credit pack purchased, used to add credits to your account exactly once.
  • Timestamps of when you first and last used the App.

The App does not:

  • Store your images or the processed results
  • Upload your design to our servers
  • Use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies
  • Use cookies
  • Sell or share your information with anyone

How the App Works

  • You select an image in your Canva design, or upload one. An uploaded image is added to your Canva uploads through Canva's official APIs.
  • The App sends our processing server a temporary link to the image, provided by Canva. The server downloads the image over a secure connection.
  • The server detects the main subject and applies the effect itself. The image is not sent to any other service for processing.
  • The result is held in the server's memory until the App collects it, then discarded. Nothing is written to disk or to a database, and any result not collected is discarded within 15 minutes.
  • When you check your balance or apply the effect, the App sends a request to our servers, identified by a token Canva issues for your account.
  • When you buy credits, the App opens a Gumroad checkout page carrying a one-time code, so the purchase can be linked back to your account.
  • If you enter a license key from a Gumroad receipt, it is sent to our credits server and checked with Gumroad to add the credits. The key you enter is not stored.

Payment Information

  • All payments are processed by Gumroad, a third-party payment processor.
  • We do not collect, store or have access to your payment card details or billing address.
  • Gumroad sends us order details for each purchase, including the email address on the order, so we can confirm the purchase and help with refunds. We use these details only to add credits and to handle support and refund requests.

Third-Party Services

  • Railway hosts our processing server, our credits server and its database.
  • Gumroad processes payments for credit purchases. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy.

We do not share data with any analytics platforms or advertising networks.

This App runs within the Canva platform. Your use of Canva (the platform) is governed by:

  • Canva's Privacy Policy
  • Canva's Terms of Use

Canva may collect data about your use of their platform according to their own privacy policy. This App does not have access to or control over Canva's data collection practices.

Data Retention

Images and results are not retained after processing. Your credit balance, credit history and purchase details are kept for as long as you use the App, so purchased credits remain available to you. You can ask us to delete them at any time, as described below.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The App is designed for general users and is not directed at children.

Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS, and every request is verified with a token issued by Canva. Access to the database is restricted to the developer.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access or delete the data associated with your Canva account. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. Deleting your data also removes any unused credits.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

International Users

The App is available globally through the Canva platform. Images are processed, and credit data is stored, on servers that may be located outside your country of residence.
