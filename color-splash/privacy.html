Privacy Policy

Color Splash

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

Overview

Color Splash ("the App") is a Canva app developed by Naveen Bhavnani that transforms images so the main subject stays in color while the background becomes grayscale.

Summary: We do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. Images are processed temporarily and immediately discarded.

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal information or user data.

The App operates within the Canva platform and:

  • Does not require user login or authentication beyond Canva
  • Does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Does not track user behavior or analytics
  • Does not use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Does not store any user data permanently

How the App Works

When you use Color Splash:

  • You select an image on your Canva design
  • Optionally, you describe which subject to keep in color
  • The image is sent to a backend server for processing
  • The processed image (with the color splash effect applied) is returned to your Canva design

Data Processing

The App processes image data as follows:

  • Your selected image is temporarily sent to our processing server via a secure connection
  • The image is processed solely to apply the color splash effect
  • The optional text prompt (subject description) is used only for processing and is not stored
  • Processed images are returned to Canva and immediately discarded from the server
  • No images, prompts, or results are retained after processing is complete

Third-Party Services

The App uses a backend processing server to apply the color splash effect. This server:

  • Only receives image data necessary for processing
  • Does not store or log any user images or prompts
  • Does not share data with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks

Canva Platform

This App runs within the Canva platform. Your use of Canva (the platform) is governed by:

  • Canva's Privacy Policy
  • Canva's Terms of Use

Canva may collect data about your use of their platform according to their own privacy policy. This App does not have access to or control over Canva's data collection practices.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The App is designed for general users and is not directed at children.

Data Security

Image data is transmitted securely between the Canva platform and our processing server. Since we do not permanently store any user data, there is no persistent user data at risk.

Your Rights

Since we do not permanently collect or store any personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete. Images and prompts exist only temporarily during processing and are discarded immediately after.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

International Users

The App is available globally through the Canva platform. Image data may be temporarily processed on servers located outside your country of residence. Since no data is stored permanently, there are no long-term cross-border data transfer concerns.
